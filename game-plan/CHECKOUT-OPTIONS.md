# Action checkout options

Actions that only need quantity and time blocks configure on the card. Actions with a selection beyond that use **View Options**. Choose the details and time blocks in one place, then use Express Checkout or Add to Cart as allowed by the action.

| Action | Additional choice | Checkout |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Get Housing | Home and location | Express or cart |
| Get Transportation | Vehicle | Express or cart |
| Join Campus Club | Club | Express or cart |
| Do an Internship | Internship focus | Express or cart |
| Study Abroad | Duration and sightseeing | Express or cart |
| Pursue Education | Degree type, field, program/major, and full-time or part-time enrollment | Express |
| Change Major | New major at the same degree level | Express |
| Get Divorced | Vehicle to keep, when there are both player and spouse vehicles | Express |

Actions requiring additional gameplay wiring are tracked in the [action audit](ACTION-AUDIT.md). Amount fields for Donate to Charity and Spend Money on Grandkids are present in the seed but are not wired to checkout yet.
